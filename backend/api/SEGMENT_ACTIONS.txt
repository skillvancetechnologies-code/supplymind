ACTION DEFINITIONS PER SEGMENT
════════════════════════════════════════

High-Performing:
  Action: Maintain relationship, increase volume
  Monitoring: Quarterly reviews
  Escalation: Account Manager → Procurement Lead
  KPIs: OTIF, Fill Rate, Quality Reject Rate

Medium-Performing:
  Action: Improvement plan with targeted support
  Monitoring: Monthly reviews
  Escalation: Account Manager → Procurement Lead
              → Supply Chain Manager
  KPIs: OTIF, Lead Time, Quality Reject Rate

Low-Performing:
  Action: Corrective action plan or replacement
  Monitoring: Weekly reviews
  Escalation: Account Manager → Procurement Lead
              → Supply Chain Director
  KPIs: OTIF, Quality Reject Rate, Lead Time, Fill Rate

Strategic:
  Action: Partnership development, long-term contracts
  Monitoring: Quarterly strategic reviews
  Escalation: Account Manager → Strategic Sourcing Lead
  KPIs: OTIF, Quality Reject Rate, Contract Value

════════════════════════════════════════
API DESIGN
════════════════════════════════════════

Endpoint 1: GET /api/analytics/segment-actions/{segment_name}
Returns action plan, monitoring frequency,
escalation path, KPIs and supplier list

Filters: ?region=<city>, ?category=<category>

Endpoint 2: PUT /api/analytics/segment-actions/{segment_name}/track
Marks action as taken with date and notes

Endpoint 3: GET /api/analytics/segment-actions/{segment_name}/status
Checks if action has been taken for a segment
