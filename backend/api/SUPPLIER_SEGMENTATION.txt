SEGMENTATION LOGIC
════════════════════════════════════════

High-Performing:
  OTIF > 85% AND Quality Reject < 2%
  AND Contract Value below median (low cost)

Medium-Performing:
  OTIF between 70-85%

Low-Performing:
  OTIF < 70% OR Quality Reject > 5%

Strategic:
  Lower OTIF/quality but small contract
  value (low volume) and acceptable
  quality — critical but small suppliers
