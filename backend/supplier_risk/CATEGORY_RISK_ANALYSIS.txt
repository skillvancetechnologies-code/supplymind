3. CATEGORY RISK INSIGHTS

---

Highest Overall Risk:
Raw Material Suppliers

Reason:
Greater exposure to quality variation and supply instability.

---

Fastest Improvement Potential:
Logistics Providers

Reason:
Operational process changes quickly improve performance metrics.

---

Most Stable Category:
Electronics Suppliers

Reason:
Long-term contracts and predictable demand patterns.

---

Urgent Attention Required:
Raw Material Suppliers

Reason:
Highest variability and strongest impact on downstream supply chain performance.

4. CATEGORY-SPECIFIC RECOMMENDATIONS

---

* Monitor capacity utilization weekly
* Track lead time deviations
* Increase forecast visibility

Raw Material Suppliers:

Component Suppliers:

* Increase quality inspections
* Track fill-rate consistency
* Monitor production schedules

Logistics Providers:

* Daily OTIF monitoring
* Route optimization
* Carrier performance reviews
