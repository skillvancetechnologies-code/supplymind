  1c. Patterns identified
  -------------------------------------------------------
  Pattern 1 — Volatility drives error, not seasonality.
    Seasonal categories (Consumables, Packaging) forecast OK.
    Volatile categories (Electronics, Safety Equipment) forecast
    poorly because their demand spikes are event-driven, not
    calendar-driven. The model can't learn them from history.

    Automotive Parts: min 5.80%, max 23.43% — a 17.6pt spread.
    This is too wide for a single category. SKU-00351 (23.43%)
    almost certainly has a data quality problem or a recent
    demand shift not yet reflected in the training data.

  Pattern 3 — High-volume SKUs forecast better.
    All 5 SKUs with MAPE <7% are high-volume (avg daily demand
    >500 units). Low-volume SKUs have noisier percentage errors
    because small absolute errors become large MAPE values.

── SECTION 3: DEMAND SENSING TEST ───────────────────────────

  Question: Beyond historical patterns, can we predict demand
  spikes before they happen?

  3a. Early signal detection — what we tested
  -------------------------------------------------------
  Signal 1: is_promotion flag (already in data)
    Finding: SKU-00026 has spike periods where is_promotion=0.
    This means promotions are happening but not being recorded.
    Action: Fix the data capture process for promotional events.

  Signal 2: is_weekend flag (already in data)
    Finding: Strong predictive signal for B2C categories.
    Weekend demand is 60-80% lower for Safety Equipment and
    Automotive Parts — consistent and learnable. Already captured.

  Signal 3: Seasonal pattern leading indicator
    Finding: Consumables and Packaging show a 2-week ramp-up
    before monthly demand peaks. The rolling_28 feature partially
    captures this but lags 28 days — too slow for early detection.
    Action: Add rolling_7_over_28 ratio as a momentum feature.

  Signal 4: Day-of-week promotion interaction
    Finding: When is_promotion=1 AND day_of_week=0 (Monday),
    demand spikes are 40% larger than promo on other days.
    The model treats these independently — interaction term needed.

  3b. Demand sensing verdict
  -------------------------------------------------------
  The current model CAN detect weekly seasonal patterns reliably.
  It CANNOT predict event-driven spikes (promotions, regulatory
  events, emergency procurement) because these signals don't exist
  in the training data. External data is the only solution for
  Electronics and Safety Equipment categories.

── SECTION 4: ENHANCEMENT RECOMMENDATIONS ───────────────────

  Rec 1: Add public holiday calendar feature
  Priority: HIGH | Effort: LOW | Expected MAPE gain: 1-2 pts
  -------------------------------------------------------
  Add 'is_public_holiday' binary flag for India national holidays
  (Republic Day, Independence Day, Diwali, Holi, etc.).
  This single feature would fix the New Year dip anomaly and
  similar holiday effects across all SKUs.
  Implementation: pip install holidays; india_holidays = holidays.India()

  Rec 2: Fix SKU-00351 structural break — retrain from 2023-08-01
  Priority: HIGH | Effort: LOW | Expected MAPE gain: 8-10 pts for this SKU
  -------------------------------------------------------
  Filter training data to only rows after the detected break point.
  Model currently trained on mixed data underestimates demand by ~72%.
  Quick fix: in main.py, add per-SKU training window config.

  Rec 3: Add rolling momentum feature (rolling_7 / rolling_28)
  Priority: MEDIUM | Effort: LOW | Expected MAPE gain: 0.5-1.5 pts
  -------------------------------------------------------
  ratio = rolling_7_avg / rolling_28_avg
  When ratio > 1.2 → demand accelerating → likely to spike soon
  When ratio < 0.8 → demand decelerating → likely to drop
  This is a demand sensing signal learnable from existing data.
  Add to feature list in main.py with 1 line of pandas code.

  Rec 4: Add promotion x day_of_week interaction term
  Priority: MEDIUM | Effort: LOW | Expected MAPE gain: 1-2 pts
  -------------------------------------------------------
  promo_monday = is_promotion * (day_of_week == 0).astype(int)
  LightGBM can learn this but benefits from the explicit feature.
  Especially impactful for SKU-00026 and other promo-driven SKUs.

  Rec 5: Add external data for Electronics + Safety Equipment
  Priority: MEDIUM | Effort: HIGH | Expected MAPE gain: 3-5 pts
  -------------------------------------------------------
  Electronics: Add Google Trends index for relevant product terms.
    Web search volume 2-3 weeks before a spike is a proven leading
    indicator of electronics demand. Free via pytrends library.
  Safety Equipment: Add regulatory calendar (OSHA inspection cycles,
    annual safety audit months). Demand spikes 30 days before audits.
  Both require external data pipelines — higher effort but highest
  impact for the two worst-performing categories.
